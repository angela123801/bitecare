# Public pages may be indexed. Signed-in areas are listed as disallowed so
# crawlers do not request them, even though the app also refuses to render
# them without a session.

# protected route requires a signed-in session and the database enforces its
# own access rules, so no patient record, report or personal detail is
# readable without one.
